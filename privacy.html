Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 24, 2026

HatchByte Studios built the Brick Blast game as a Free app. This Service is provided by HatchByte Studios at no cost and is intended for use as is.

This page is used to inform visitors regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of information if anyone decides to use our Service.

Information Collection and Use

We do not ask for your real name, email address, age, or other contact details. The only player information we store online is described in the "Online Leaderboard" section below.

We also use third-party services that may collect certain information as described below.

In-App Purchases

Brick Blast offers optional in-app purchases, processed entirely through Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment information — all payment details are handled directly by Google Play. Purchases grant in-game currency and content only.

Advertising

The game shows a banner ad on the main menu and optional rewarded video ads (for example, "watch an ad to get a free spin"). Ads are served through Unity LevelPlay (formerly ironSource) and its ad networks, including ironSource Ads and Unity Ads. These services may collect certain information to serve and personalize ads and measure ad performance. This may include:

  • Advertising ID and device identifiers
  • Type of device and operating system
  • IP address and general location (e.g., country or region level)
  • Ad interaction and engagement data

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

For more information, please review their privacy policy:

  • Unity Privacy Policy (Unity LevelPlay, ironSource Ads, Unity Ads)

Cloud Save (Google Play Games)

If you sign in with Google Play Games, we use Google Play Games Services to save your progress to your Google account so it can be restored if you reinstall the app or switch devices. This is limited to your game progress and does not include any additional personal information. See Google's Privacy Policy for details on how Google Play Games handles your account data:

  • Google Privacy Policy

Online Leaderboard

When you are signed in with Google Play Games, your best score is submitted to our online leaderboard so it can be shown to other players. For this we store:

  • Your player name: the name you choose in the game, or your Google Play Games display name if you have not chosen one
  • Your best score
  • A random player ID generated by the game (it is not linked to your Google account ID)

This data is stored using Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and anonymous Firebase Authentication) and is visible to other players on the leaderboard. To have your leaderboard entry removed, contact us at the email address below.

  • Firebase Privacy and Security

Gameplay Data

We store your progress, score, in-game currency, and settings (such as volume) locally on your device. This data is not shared with or transmitted to any third party, except as described above for cloud save and the online leaderboard.

Children's Privacy

This game is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal data, please contact us so we can take action.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify users of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
